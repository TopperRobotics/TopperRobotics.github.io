Robotics at Science Hill

Topper Robotics is Science Hill High School's robotics club. We've been FIRST Robotics Competition Team 3984 since our rookie year in 2012.

Our mission is to give every student hands-on experience in STEM. Members learn programming, machining, design and construction, and they help run the team itself, from public relations to business management and logistics.

We're committed to equal opportunity for every student, regardless of socioeconomic status or background.

Our 2026 robot

In REBUILT, the 2026 FRC game, robots gather yellow foam balls called Fuel, shoot them into their alliance's Hub, and climb a ladder-shaped Tower at the end of the match.


Tuanonator

The Tuanonator is our swerve-drive robot that uses both the trench and the ramp to get around the field. It carries about 25 Fuel per cycle and uses a Limelight with AprilTags to track its position anywhere on the field.

Robot specs
Drivetrain SDS MK4i L2 swerve
Weight 88.4 lb without bumpers and battery
Size 24 × 24 × 22 in
Fuel capacity About 25 per cycle
Motors NEO 2.0 (intake), NEO Vortex (drivetrain and shooter), NEO 1.1 (feeder), NEO 550 (belt)
Autonomous Yes, with full-field localization
Vision Limelight with AprilTags
Software Java in VS Code
On the field Uses the trench and the ramp, and can play defense

What a season looks like

FIRST is a nonprofit that runs robotics programs for K–12 students around the world. In FRC, teams work under strict rules with limited time and resources to raise funds, build a team brand, and design, build and program an industrial-size robot. It's the closest to real-world engineering a student can get.

  1. Jan

    Kickoff

    FIRST reveals the new game to every team on the same day. We read the game manual and start sketching.

  2. Jan–Mar

    Build

    Prototype, design in CAD, machine parts, wire the electronics and write the code. Then practice driving.

  3. Mar–Apr

    Compete

    Regionals like Smoky Mountains in Sevierville and Rocket City in Huntsville: about ten qualification matches with assigned partners, then alliance selection and playoffs.

  4. May–Dec

    Offseason

    Training new members, outreach with younger students, and building VEX robots.
